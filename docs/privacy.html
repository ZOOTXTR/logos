💎 Logos Privacy Policy

Published by ZOVTEX

Last updated: July 2026

1. Data Storage

All game data (level, XP, achievements, Gem balance, settings) is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. We do not collect or store personal data on our servers.

2. Personal Information

Playing Logos does not require registration. You can play anonymously. If you choose to link an email for cloud sync, it is stored locally and used only for backup purposes.

3. Cloud Sync

Cloud save uses Firebase Firestore. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. You can unlink your account at any time, which removes the cloud association.

4. In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed securely via Google Play Billing and Apple App Store IAP. No payment information is handled by the app.

5. Third-Party Services

This app uses:

  • Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Cloud Messaging) — for authentication, cloud sync, and push notifications
  • Sentry — for crash reporting and error monitoring
  • Expo — for OTA updates and build infrastructure

6. Contact

For questions or data deletion requests: support@zovtex.com
